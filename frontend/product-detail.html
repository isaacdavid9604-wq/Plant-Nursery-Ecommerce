How to Keep Your Plant Thriving

Lighting Dynamics

Position within 3 to 6 feet of an east or south-facing window. Avoid harsh scorching direct noon sunlight which can burn delicate foliage.

Soil Hydration Rule

Insert your index finger 2 inches into the soil. Water thoroughly only when the top layer feels bone dry. Always empty the bottom drip tray.

Humidity & Cleaning

Wipe leaves gently with a damp microfiber cloth monthly to clean dust and allow the plant to maximize chlorophyll photosynthesis.

Recommended Soil & Repotting Schedule

Our nursery plants arrive in an airy, aerated blend containing coconut coir, perlite, organic worm castings, and pine bark. We recommend repotting every 18 to 24 months into a container that is approximately 2 inches wider in diameter.

Engineered for Safe Botanical Transit

Every living plant is locked into place using corrugated cardboard collars that hold the root ball securely without spilling soil. Roots are wrapped in moisture-preserving coco wraps, ensuring the plant stays hydrated for up to 7 days in transit.
