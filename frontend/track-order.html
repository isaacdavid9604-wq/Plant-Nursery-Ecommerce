How We Deliver Living Plants Safely

Every botanical specimen undergoes a strict 5-point acclimatization and hydration protocol before courier handover.

1. Root Hydration

Roots receive deep nutrient hydration 4 hours prior to packaging.

2. Bio-Safe Cushioning

3. Breathable Vents
